Olympia: Space Trading

A 3D space station orbiting Jupiter where five AI agents live and work, each themed on part of the Jovian system: Io, Europa, the aurora, the Great Red Spot and Ganymede. They walk to their holographic consoles when a task comes in, sleep when idle and look annoyed on hard ones. Next, they'll run a real multi-agent trading research team.

Voxway

A voxel self-driving car simulator: Crossy Road, flipped. The self-driving car is the one in traffic, and pedestrians hop across the road in front of it, so it has to predict them, yield, merge and get through intersections. It exports Waymo-style scenarios to train pedestrian-prediction models.
